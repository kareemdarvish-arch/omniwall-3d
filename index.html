3D Printing Material Selection Guide

Mechanical properties and recommendations for workshop wall longevity

PETG (Polyethylene Terephthalate Glycol) BEST CHOICE FOR WORKSHOPS

Max Temp: 75°C

Combines the ease of printing with high impact resistance and chemical endurance. Highly resistant to automotive grease, lubricating oils, WD-40, and solvents. Does not shatter under shock load when heavy tools are dropped into mounts.

Infill: 25% Gyroid • Density: 1.27 g/cm³ • Elongation at Break: High

PLA+ (Tough Polylactic Acid) ECONOMICAL & RIGID

Max Temp: 55°C

Highest stiffness and crispiest visual layer appearance. Environmentally conscious cornstarch-derived bio-polymer. Excellent for air-conditioned indoor workshops, makerspaces, electronics benches, and craft rooms.

Infill: 20% Grid • Density: 1.24 g/cm³ • Tensile Strength: 50 MPa

Carbon Fiber Reinforced PETG INDUSTRIAL PRO

Max Temp: 85°C

Doped with 15% chopped high-modulus carbon fiber strands. Eliminates flex under heavy leverage (such as large socket bars and drills). Produces a stunning matte textured finish that hides layer lines completely.

Infill: 30% Honeycomb • Density: 1.30 g/cm³ • Flexural Modulus: Extreme
